# Connect your business

Welcome to the neargo Developer Center. Choose your business area, then work through authentication, request conventions, and integration workflows.

> New to neargo? Start with the [quick start](getting-started/quick-start.md).

## Choose a business area

| Area | Documentation | Typical use cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food | [Food overview](food/overview.md) | Menus, ordering, kitchen fulfillment, checkout |
| Retail | [Retail overview](retail/overview.md) | Products, orders, inventory, after-sales |
| Services | [Services overview](services/overview.md) | Bookings, work orders, fulfillment |
| Payments | [Payment overview](payments/overview.md) | Collections, queries, refunds, notifications |

## Integration path

1. Confirm the capabilities and authorization you need.
2. Obtain the connection details and test identity for your environment.
3. Read the authentication and request rules for your business area.
4. Verify the workflow and failure recovery in an isolated environment.
5. Complete production acceptance checks.

## Using the documentation

Browse the navigation or search by keyword. Replace example environment addresses and identity parameters with your authorized values.
