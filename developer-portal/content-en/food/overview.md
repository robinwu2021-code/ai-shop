# Food overview

## Business scope

Food capabilities cover menus, ordering, kitchen fulfillment, and checkout. Confirm your workflow, caller identity, and store scope before integrating.

## Prepare your integration

1. Identify your use case and the required API scope.
2. Confirm third-party authorization and an isolated test environment.
3. Follow the formal external contract for identity, amounts, states, and idempotency.

## Published documentation

This page introduces the integration area. Full external API documentation is being prepared within the authorized scope. Merchant apps, consumer clients, and internal services do not automatically share the third-party API contract.
